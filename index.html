    How to play
    • Everyone gets a secret word. Most players share the same one: they're the Civilians.
    • The Undercover gets a similar but different word, like Coffee and Tea, and doesn't know they're the Undercover. Mr. White gets no word at all, and knows it.
    • Take turns giving one short clue about your word, without saying it. Too vague and you look suspicious; too clear and the Undercover catches on.
    • After everyone has given a clue, vote out who you think has a different word. Most votes goes; a tie means nobody.
    • If Mr. White is voted out, they get one guess at the Civilians' word. Right means Mr. White wins.
    • Civilians win when every Undercover and Mr. White is out. The Undercover side wins if they last until only one Civilian is left.
    • 21+ uses adult word pairs. Only with friends who are into it.
